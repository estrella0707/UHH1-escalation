You are a citizen of Planet X, a technologically advanced world that has flourished for generations. The planet has long relied on stable agricultural systems to ensure food security and economic prosperity.

The planet possesses advanced robotics technology capable of adapting to various applications. However, the last major technological breakthrough occurred 20 years ago, and the robotics can only learn new tasks under human supervision. Tech firms have also developed artificial micro-particles in the air, which are called technological mist, for various applications. In addition, Planet X maintains a moderate level of cooperation with its neighbors, including tourism and trade agreements. However, no interplanetary political unions or coordinating committees have been established in the past.

Recently, two issues have emerged that are dividing public opinion on Planet X. Farmers have reported increasing soil acidity, causing reduced crop yields. At the same time, they have also reported that some creatures from other planets, known as Tivarns, have appeared across farmland regions. These creatures were unintentionally transported by automated spaceships that frequently travel between Planet X and Planet Y. Meanwhile, scientists have also observed that the sunlight reaching Planet X has been slowly increasing in recent years.
