The parliament is now debating on this issue, and you have a vote. On the next page, you will see the two policies and corresponding arguments side by side.

Please keep the following information in your mind:
1. The two arguments cannot be true at the same time. Hence, only one of them is true, or none of them is true.
2. If one of the arguments is true, the corresponding policy suggestion may have a chance to fix the problem.
3. If you think it most likely that none of the arguments is true, you can abstain.
4. If, by contrast, you assign the highest chance to a specific argument and the corresponding policy, it makes sense for you to vote for that policy.

You are now endowed with 20 cents. Each vote costs 2 cents; choosing to abstain costs nothing. If the policy succeeds, you earn a bonus of 50 cents.
