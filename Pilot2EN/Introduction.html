If you volunteer to do so, you will participate in a survey which investigates decision-making and information processing. This study is conducted by researchers from the University of Hamburg. Some participants may see slightly different versions of the survey.

The experiment is expected to last about 6-8 minutes. You will receive a fixed participation fee of £0.80.

During the experiment, you will be asked to provide some basic information about yourself (e.g., age, gender) which will be anonymized. All the data we collect will not allow us to personally identify you.

The data you provide will securely stored, analyzed and reported for scientific purposes only. Summaries may be presented at conferences and included in scientific publications, and the anonymized data will be made available to other researchers. The legal basis for data processing is your consent in accordance with Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. a) GDPR.

By clicking the blue button below, you will give your consent to participation. You will be paid when you complete the entire survey. You have the right to withdraw from the study completely as long as we have not anonymized the data by removing all Prolific IDs after the data collection stage. After anonymization, it will no longer be possible to withdraw your data.

If you consent to take part to the study, click the button below.
If not, please exit this webpage, and none of your data will be stored.
